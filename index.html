How to play Phone: drag on the left to move. Touch and drag on the right to shoot: the bullet flies the way you drag, and drag further to shoot further.
Computer: WASD or arrows to move, hold the mouse to shoot at your cursor (Space also shoots).
Special: collect purple orbs and deal damage to fill your meter, then tap the screen (E on computer) to unleash it. Domain Expansion needs a meter twice as big as the other two.
Pads: yellow pads give a speed boost, green pads heal you slowly when you are not under fire.
Multiplayer: one person hosts and shares the room code; the host must keep this tab open.
